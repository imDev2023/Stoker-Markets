# stonk-fun-solana

An "open + trustless" asset-paired Solana launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab named Stoker (ticker STOKER, domain `stoker.markets`; the packages keep their `@launchpad/` scope), built after researching StonkFun.
No custom on-chain program: our own LaunchLab `PlatformConfig` plus a Cloudflare backend and a Next.js app.

## Code map

- `packages/chain` - LaunchLab and CPMM PDAs, instruction builders from the IDLs (Anchor coder), launch, curve and pool trades (transfer-fee aware, `transferFee.ts`; referral share fee), migration, decoder. Server-only.
- `packages/db` - Drizzle schema and migrations (`pnpm --filter @launchpad/db generate`).
- `packages/contract` - zod API contract shared by the API and every client.
- `packages/arweave` - ANS-104 data items signed with ed25519 (the id is known at signing) and ArDrive Turbo's few HTTP endpoints (upload, price, balance, SOL top-up). Used by the API and by the stand-in in `tools/stack`.
- `apps/api` - Hono Worker: prepare endpoints, Helius webhook indexer (which also adds graduated pools to the webhook through Helius's API, `src/helius.ts`, when `HELIUS_API_KEY` and `HELIUS_WEBHOOK_ID` are set), Quote Risk Labels (`src/quotes/labels.ts`: from the mint's authorities and extensions and the token list, re-read by the Indexer when a delivered transaction marks a quote mint writable), Copy Protection (`src/copies`: the folded name and ticker keys, the image's difference hash, and the check that `POST /v1/launches/check` answers and `prepare` refuses with 409 inside a launched coin's window, `COPY_PROTECTION_SECONDS`, seven days; the Indexer stores every launch's keys and labels a copy; ADR 0012), read API (the reference is the site's `/api` page; `docs/api.md` points at it), metadata hosting, API keys (`src/apiKeys.ts`), the operator view and fee-change announcements (`src/routes/operator.ts`), and a Cron Trigger (`scheduled`) with five jobs: the graduation watch (plus `reward-tax-lock`, `src/rewardTax.ts`, and the Metadata Upload, `src/metadata.ts`, job `metadata-upload`: posts every launched coin's image and metadata JSON, signed at prepare time, to Turbo, topping credits up with SOL from the operations wallet when refused with 402; also run right after a launch is indexed; ADR 0008), the Quote Registry refresh, the Fee Collector (`src/fees`: claims revenue into the operations wallet, sweeps it to the treasury less the Burn's unspent budgets and what Creators are owed, writes the Ledger) followed on the same cron by the Creator Forward (`src/fees/forward.ts`, job `creator-forward`: pays each Creator their share of collected revenue per Quote Asset, one `creator_forwards` row and one Ledger entry per payment; the shares are computed in `src/fees/creatorShares.ts`), the Reward Engine (`src/rewards`: harvests Reward Tax, snapshots holders, pays rounds) and the Burn and Flywheel (`src/burn`). Every job run is recorded in `job_runs` (`GET /v1/operator/jobs`); a failed run raises a `job_failed` alert, resolved by the next success.

## Settled (do not re-litigate)

- LaunchLab checks a trade's `minimum_amount_out` against what reaches the wallet after the received asset's Token-2022 transfer fee (proven on the fork with a Reward Launch's coin and the taxed quote): quote limits from the wallet-side figure, never the pre-fee one, or the program fails with 6004.
- A Reward Launch (#16) is `initialize_with_token_2022` with a transfer fee at 1%, 3% or 5%; the fee-config authority is LaunchLab's vault authority until migration, then the operations wallet, which the `reward-tax-lock` job revokes after Graduation so the tax can never change; the withdraw-withheld authority is the operations wallet from launch. Curve rule group 0 pins standard coins, group 1 Reward Launches.
- Creator earnings (ADR 0009, #85, owner 2026-10-08): StonkFun's split, 1.00% Platform Fee and 0% Creator Fee on the curve; the Creator's share is half of what the platform collects on their coin's trades on either venue (half of each curve trade's Platform Fee, half of each pool creator fee collection, each rounded down on its own), forwarded in the Quote Asset, a Reward Launch like any coin, once worth `CREATOR_FORWARD_MIN_LAMPORTS`; nothing to claim, no claim UI or endpoint, and the Indexer tracks no claims. A share is owed only once its fee is collected (a curve trade when a Platform Fee claim for its Quote Asset landed at or after its slot), and what is owed counts every forward that is `sending` or `paid`, so nothing is paid twice. The user-facing name is Creator Earnings (`GET /v1/creators/:address/earnings`); "Creator Fee" is LaunchLab's on-chain term only, which the fee-change flow still names. The creator page is the connected wallet's own (owner, 2026-10-08): `/creator` only, no nav or footer entry, reached from the account menu once the wallet has launched a coin, and nothing links to a Creator's page (the Ledger shows each forward's wallet on the explorer).
- Reward Rounds and the Burn's budgets: `docs/adr/0006-reward-rounds-and-burn-budgets.md`. Burn and Flywheel budgets are shares of the Ledger's revenue, net of the Creators' share (ADR 0009), less what earlier burns spent; the Fee Collector's sweep leaves the unspent budgets in the operations wallet. A payout's own Reward Tax is withheld again, so a paid round can open the next one.
- The Payout Asset (#43, ADR 0006 amendment): a Reward Launch pays its holders in the coin or in its Quote Asset, chosen on the launch form, kept in `launch_drafts.reward_payout`, copied to `launches` by the Indexer (a Reward Launch made without our API pays in the coin), never updated. For a Quote Asset payout the Reward Engine sells one slice per run into the coin's own market, at most `REWARD_SALE_MAX_RESERVE_BPS` (0.5%) of its coin reserve with a 1% slippage limit (`apps/api/src/rewards/sales.ts`), writes each sale to `reward_sales` before sending, records it as two `reward_sale` Ledger entries and indexes it as a trade; a round opens once the coins sold since the last round reach the rules' threshold and pays the pot (landed proceeds less what was paid) in the Quote Asset, SOL as plain SOL. The sweep leaves SOL pots behind (`rewardPotLamports`); token pots are never swept. A SOL payout under the rent minimum to a wallet with no SOL is `returned` to the pot and the round finishes without it.
- The treasury is an eligible holder in Reward Rounds (owner, 2026-10-04): it holds coins swept from pool fees and takes its share, and the rewards page says so. It is a plain file keypair (not a hardware wallet, owner 2026-10-06), so `eligibleHolders` counts it; a program-owned vault would be skipped. Excluding it later is an announced rule change, only if the community asks.
- The platform's keys (owner, 2026-10-06; prefix shortened 2026-10-09): admin, Treasury, operations wallet and the Platform Token's mint are four file keypairs whose addresses start with `Stoke` (a prefix, visible in truncated addresses, and the brand's verb; `Stoker` was the plan until the grind was measured at 18 days a key, see the trap below, and a suffix as well is computationally out of reach), ground on the owner's Mac into `~/.config/stoker/mainnet/` (runbook section 1 has the command, the log and the rate; all four landed on 2026-10-10 and were assigned on 2026-10-13: admin `Stoke7675...`, operations `StokehV2b...`, treasury `StokezuRE...`, Platform Token mint `StokeDgBj...`, the full addresses in the runbook's section 1 and on #23; the mainnet PlatformConfig and Platform Lookup Table were created the same day, so go-live items 2 to 4 are done and the plain keypair files leave the Mac after the API deploy). Launches are open to anyone from the first deploy: no gate (owner, 2026-10-14, #97 closed), and the platform goes live after 2026-10-25. The Platform Token launches through the chosen-mint-key path (#75, merged: `POST /v1/operator/mint-keys`, runbook section 10); every other coin keeps a derived address until #73.
- Copy Protection (owner, 2026-10-26, #120 item 1, ADR 0012): for seven days after a coin launches (`COPY_PROTECTION_SECONDS`, which may become thirty) nobody else may launch a coin with its name, ticker or image; afterwards anyone may and the coin gets a label naming the original. Near copies (one edit from five characters, BABY or MINI in front) are refused inside the window too; tickers stay ASCII. Not decided: a Quote Asset's own ticker (refuse always, proposed) and Jupiter-verified tickers (label only, proposed).
- Referral share fee (#21): a trade names `share_fee_rate` and the referrer's Quote Asset account (the account before the system program among the remaining accounts); the rate is capped by the GlobalConfig's `max_share_fee_rate` (SOL 1%, most configs including every stock 0), so `referralShare` on a Quote Asset says whether it applies. The Platform Fee and Creator Fee shown anywhere come from the chain (`GET /v1/platform`), never a constant; a change is announced ahead (#22) and applied by the cold admin key.

- After Graduation a trader pays 1.25% and the platform collects about 1.16% of it: 95% of the pool's 1% creator fee (mainnet's config keeps 5% of it for Raydium, its `creator_fee_share_rate`, found when the local chain took that config on 2026-10-25; half of what arrives is forwarded to the Creator, so the Creator's exact share after Graduation is 0.475%, said as about 0.5% everywhere but the launch summary's note, owner's choice) and, through the Fee Key on the locked liquidity, the liquidity providers' 84% of the 0.25% trade fee; Raydium keeps 0.04%. The mainnet CPMM config is recorded in the runbook's section 2. StonkFun's own token trades mostly on Meteora, but its launchpad is LaunchLab like ours.
- StonkFun is a LaunchLab `PlatformConfig` pair plus an off-chain backend; its ~0.5% creator share is an off-chain forward that broke for ~3 weeks (`research/social/telegram-group-insights.md` in the private repo `imDev2023/Stoker-Markets-research`).
