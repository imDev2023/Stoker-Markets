## Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

This notice explains what data using StonkFun produces, what the site stores in your browser, and what is done with it. It is the Privacy Policy the StonkFun Website Terms of Use refer to (section 17) and forms part of them; "StonkFun" and "the Terms" here mean what the Terms define them to mean, and a section number refers to the Terms.

### What is collected

StonkFun does not ask for your name, email, or identity. Using StonkFun still produces data it handles:

- Wallet addresses and transactions. These are public on the Solana blockchain whether or not you use this site.

- Content you submit when launching: name, ticker, image, description, and links. Written to permanent storage at launch and cannot be deleted by anyone.

- Your IP address, the country and region the hosting provider derives from it, and your browser and device details, read while a request is handled for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and the Restricted User rule in section 4 of the Terms. StonkFun does not store your IP address. Rate limiting keeps a one-way hash of it, not the address, for about an hour.

- A record of your acceptance of the Terms: version, time, country and region, your browser identification string, and, if a wallet was connected and you signed the acceptance message it showed you, your wallet address and that signature. Declining to sign records the acceptance without a wallet. The record holds no IP address.

- Server logs of requests to the site and the API, held by the hosting provider.

### Cookies and browser storage

The site sets the following. None of them track you across other websites.

- stonkfun_terms: records which version of the Terms you accepted. Kept for one year. Required; without it the acceptance notice returns on every visit.

- Google Analytics cookies (names beginning _ga): page-view analytics, loaded only after you accept the Terms and only where analytics is enabled on the deployment. Google processes this data under its own terms.

- Admin session cookie: set only for platform staff who sign in to the admin panel. Visitors never receive it.

- Session storage: a launch in progress is kept in your browser tab so a page reload can resume it, and the integrator-docs password is kept for the tab if you enter one; platform staff who sign in to the admin panel also have their session kept in the tab. All of it is cleared when the tab closes.

- Wallet connection: the site stores the name of the last wallet you connected and whether you have connected before, in browser storage on this site, until you clear it. Your wallet extension may also keep its own data, controlled by the extension.

- Jupiter swap widget: the swap widget on token pages keeps your swap settings in browser storage on this site under keys beginning stonkfun-swap. The widget script loads on every page from Jupiter’s servers.

### How it is used

To run the platform: preparing launch and claim transactions for your wallet to sign, showing token pages, computing creator shares and holder distributions, and answering API calls.

To keep it safe: rate limiting, refusing access from the sanctioned places named in the Restricted User rule in section 4 of the Terms, preventing abuse, and investigating incidents.

To meet legal duties: keeping records StonkFun may need to answer a regulator or court, and to enforce the Terms.

### Who sees it

The providers StonkFun relies on to run the platform: hosting, database, RPC and blockchain data providers, key management, and analytics. They process data in other countries, including ones where StonkFun is not offered.

Third parties your browser contacts directly: Jupiter (its swap widget script loads on every page and receives your IP address; your wallet address when you swap through it or a dev-buy is funded through it), the Irys gateway and an image proxy (wsrv.nl) that serve token images, and Google where analytics is enabled. Content you supply at launch is sent to Irys for permanent storage on Arweave.

Authorities where the law requires, including a regulator or court, and anyone StonkFun needs to involve to investigate abuse or enforce the Terms.

StonkFun does not sell data.

### How long it is kept

Acceptance and security records are kept for as long as StonkFun needs them to enforce the Terms and answer lawful requests. Rate-limit counters, keyed by a one-way hash of the address rather than the address itself, are deleted about an hour after their window closes. Server logs are held by the hosting provider for its standard retention period. Blockchain and permanent-storage data cannot be deleted by StonkFun or anyone; StonkFun can only remove it from what the site displays.

### Your choices

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser at any time; the acceptance notice will then return. You can block analytics with a browser setting or extension. You can disconnect your wallet at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data StonkFun holds; contact StonkFun through the channels named in section 18 of the Terms.
