# Revenue

The platform automatically claims its trading fees. Approximately 60% of the platform revenue is used to buy $STONK on the open market and burn it. The rest is retained.

Note: due to pricing volatile custom quote tokens, pricing of exact splits may slightly vary. Using $STONK as a quote token executes a burn without a buyback.
