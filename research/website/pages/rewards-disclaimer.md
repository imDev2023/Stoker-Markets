# Rewards Disclaimer

Holder rewards are a mechanical feature of the tokens that carry them, not an investment product, dividend, revenue share, or yield. Holding a token gives you no right to any distribution, amount, or schedule, and no claim on the rewards wallet or anything in it.

Figures on the rewards page and on token pages are historical and depend on trading having happened. They are valued at current prices, not at the price when each distribution landed, and are not a promise, projection, or basis for expecting any future distribution.

A distribution is normally paid in the token the launch is paired against. When the platform holds a large share of a coin's circulating supply, a round may instead pay holders in that coin itself. Which asset a round pays in is decided by the system at the time, is not a choice you make, and is not guaranteed either way.

Distributions can be delayed or halted at any time, including by automated safety checks, and the program can be changed or discontinued prospectively. Eligibility is measured at each distribution, and distributions go to whichever wallet holds the tokens at that moment.

Collection, sale, and distribution run on automated systems that depend on Solana, Raydium, Jupiter, and price feeds StonkFun does not control. Any step can fail or be delayed, and nothing is owed for what did not land. See the Terms of Service, section 5.
