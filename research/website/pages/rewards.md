# Rewards

Rewards distributed to holders in whatever their coin is paired against — or in the coin itself.

Reward tokens distribute to holders pro-rata in the quote token the launch is paired against — or in the coin itself, when the platform holds a large share of its supply. Newer (v3) launches are funded by a transfer tax on every transfer (1% or 3%, set at launch), earlier ones by approx. 85% of their 4% pool's trading fees. Values are shown at each token's current price — not its price when the payout landed.
